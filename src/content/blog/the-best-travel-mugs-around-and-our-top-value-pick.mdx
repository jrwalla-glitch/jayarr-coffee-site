A travel mug has two jobs: keeping coffee hot and keeping it in the mug. In October 2020 we compared eight popular travel mugs, tumblers and thermoses for a video, from a tumbler listed at $12 to a heated mug listed at $180, and named a top overall pick, a best value and a high-tech pick. This page keeps those verdicts, adds what each maker publishes about its mug today, checked October 18, 2026, and says which ones are still sold.

## What to look for

**A lid that suits how you carry it.** The makers here say plainly which lids seal. Contigo calls its AutoSeal lid leak- and spill-proof, with a lock for the button. YETI says the magnet in its tumbler's MagSlider lid "is not leakproof and will not prevent spills," and that its tumblers aren't meant to be packed, because the lid will leak. None of the other tumbler makers calls its lid leakproof: Sunwill's is "spill proof," the Beast's "spill-resistant" and Corkcicle's "shatterproof." If the mug goes in a bag, that difference matters more than anything else on this page.

**Heat retention, as each maker rates it.** Contigo rates its original West Loop and Luxe at up to 5 hours hot, Juro its 20 oz tumbler at up to 6 hours, Sunwill its at over 6, and Corkcicle its tumbler at 3 hours hot and 9 or more cold. YETI and Greens Steel, the maker of the Beast, give no hours at all. None of these makers' pages says how its hours were measured, such as how hot the coffee started or whether the lid stayed shut, so treat each figure as that maker's own rating rather than a ranking. You can help any of them: Breville's coffee-maker manual suggests filling a cup or travel mug with hot water for about 30 seconds before brewing into it, and Jessica Easto writes in *Craft Coffee: A Manual* that a good preheated thermos can keep coffee surprisingly fresh for several hours, though below about 175°F&nbsp;(79°C) it turns increasingly sour or bitter.

**A lid you can clean.** Lids collect coffee oils. Corkcicle's gasket slips off and its slider pops out, and it suggests vinegar or baking soda and lemon juice for the lid; Contigo's lids open up for cleaning. Check the dishwasher rules for each part: Contigo says the original West Loop's lid can go on the dishwasher's top rack but its body should be washed by hand, while YETI, Corkcicle and Greens Steel call their tumblers dishwasher safe.

**The size you'll fill.** A 16 fl oz mug holds about 473 ml and a 20 fl oz tumbler about 591 ml (calculated). A full 20 oz tumbler holds as much as two and a half 8 fl oz cups (calculated); there's more on what that means for caffeine below.

## For a bag or a commute: a lid that seals itself

### Contigo AutoSeal West Loop and Luxe: our top overall pick

**Our 2020 verdict:** the AutoSeal is all in the name. You press the button to sip and release it when you're done, and the mouthpiece seals itself again between sips. The mug stays cool to the touch on the outside whatever the temperature inside. The West Loop and the Luxe are two versions of Contigo's AutoSeal, and the West Loop looks more like a traditional travel mug. Very few owners seemed to have complaints, the build quality felt quite nice, and we marked both versions our top overall picks, listed at $20 for the West Loop and $25 for the Luxe in our video.

**A correction:** the video said the West Loop could go right in the dishwasher. Contigo says its lid is top-rack dishwasher safe and its body should be washed by hand.

**Now:** Contigo still sells the original West Loop, 16 oz (model 2074750), listed at $29.49 when checked. Contigo calls its AutoSeal lid leak- and spill-proof, with a button lock, and says it keeps drinks hot up to 5 hours or cold up to 12; the lid opens up completely for cleaning; and the mug fits most car cup holders and sits under most single-serve brewers, at 3.5 in wide and 7.8 in tall. Contigo also sells two newer West Loops: the 2.0 in 20 oz, which it rates at up to 7 hours hot, and the 3.0 in 16 oz, at up to 11 hours. The Luxe 16 oz (model 2063408), which Contigo describes with an elevated metallic finish, was listed at $39.49, rated at up to 5 hours hot or 12 cold, with a lock and a lid whose underside opens for cleaning, and Contigo calls both its lid and body top-rack dishwasher safe. Both carry Contigo's limited lifetime guarantee. To buy: [Contigo's West Loop](https://www.gocontigo.com/mugs/west-loop-stainless-steel-travel-mug-with-autoseal-lid-16oz/SAP_2074750.html) and [Luxe](https://www.gocontigo.com/mugs/contigo-luxe-travel-mug-with-autoseal-lid-16oz/SAP_2063408.html) pages, or the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Contigo+Autoseal+West+Loop+Travel+Mug&tag=jayarrcoffee-20" rel="sponsored noopener">Contigo AutoSeal West Loop</a> on Amazon, where the results mix the original, 2.0 and 3.0 versions.

## For the car or the desk: tumblers with sliding lids

### YETI Rambler 20 oz Tumbler

**Our 2020 verdict:** what would a list of travel mugs be without YETI? Fans swear by its ability to keep drinks hot or cold for a long time, and the Rambler pairs YETI's insulation with a MagSlider lid, whose magnets make it easy to open and close tightly. It's dishwasher safe, its coated exterior keeps its color, and it comes in plenty of colors. Owners generally seemed pleased, which made it easy to recommend, though some complained that it isn't completely leakproof. It was listed at $30 in our video.

**Now:** YETI's UK store listed the Rambler 20 oz (591 ml) Tumbler with MagSlider Lid at £30, in stock, when checked; on Amazon, the YETI Authorized seller listed it at $35. YETI describes double-wall vacuum insulation and kitchen-grade 18/8 stainless steel, calls the tumbler and lid dishwasher safe and gives a five-year warranty. It confirms the leak complaint: the MagSlider magnet "is not leakproof and will not prevent spills," and tumblers aren't meant to be packed. To buy: YETI's own stores, or the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=YETI+Rambler+20+oz+Tumbler&tag=jayarrcoffee-20" rel="sponsored noopener">YETI Rambler 20 oz Tumbler</a> on Amazon, where a few unrelated tumblers appear as ads among the YETI results.

### Juro Tumbler 20 oz: our best value

**Our 2020 verdict:** a vacuum-insulated 20 oz tumbler with lids and a steel straw, plus a brush to clean it. It suits hot coffee and cold brew alike, and the straw makes iced coffee easy. Owners seemed to like its materials and design, and complaints were few and far between. At around twelve dollars it cost far less than most of the list for most of the performance, which made it our best value; the one downside was that it came only in black.

**Now:** Juro sells through Amazon, where both of its 20 oz tumbler listings, including the one our video linked, read "Currently unavailable," with no date for its return, when checked October 18, 2026. Juro's listing rates it at up to 6 hours hot and up to 24 hours cold, with a slider lid and a flip lid. With no listing you can buy from, there's no link here.

### Sunwill 20 oz Coffee Tumbler

**Our 2020 verdict:** a hefty, double-walled stainless-steel tumbler with a sweatproof finish and a wide choice of colors, and owners seemed very impressed by its splash- and spill-proof lid. It had no single standout feature, but almost every feature you'd look for, and at fifteen dollars in 2020 it was a hard-to-beat value.

**Now:** Sunwill sells through Amazon, where its 20 oz Coffee Tumbler, the one in our video, was in stock at $13.99, sold by Sunwill US, when checked. Sunwill's listing describes double-wall vacuum insulation, 18/8 (304) stainless steel, hot drinks kept hot for over 6 hours and cold for around 24, a BPA-free sliding lid with a silicone gasket that it calls spill-proof, a fit for most cup holders, and a dishwasher-safe tumbler. To buy: the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=SUNWILL+Coffee+Tumbler+20+oz+Insulated+Travel+Mug&tag=jayarrcoffee-20" rel="sponsored noopener">Sunwill 20 oz Coffee Tumbler</a> on Amazon; the first few results are ads, including other Sunwill tumblers.

### Corkcicle Classic Tumbler 16 oz

**Our 2020 verdict:** its main distinguishing feature is triple-walled stainless-steel insulation, where most insulated mugs use two walls. Corkcicle also calls it shatterproof, and it comes in sleek black, walnut wood and a variety of other colors. A great all-around option at a somewhat premium price, listed at $30 in our video. Some owners warned against putting it in the dishwasher, so we suggested washing it by hand.

**Now:** Corkcicle listed the Classic Tumbler at $30 for 16 oz when checked, with 12 and 24 oz sizes too. It describes triple insulation, cold for 9 or more hours and hot for 3, a sliding, shatterproof lid, a silicone base that stays put and a fit for cup holders, and lists the current Classic Tumbler as dishwasher safe. For an older tumbler, follow the care instructions for its particular model and finish. On the current tumbler, the lid gasket slips off and the slider pops out for cleaning, and Corkcicle gives a 1-year warranty. To buy: [Corkcicle's store](https://corkcicle.com/products/classic-tumbler), or the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Corkcicle+Classic+Tumbler+16+oz&tag=jayarrcoffee-20" rel="sponsored noopener">Corkcicle Classic Tumbler 16 oz</a> on Amazon.

### Beast 20 oz Tumbler

**Our 2020 verdict:** a decent all-around tumbler with great insulation, a sweat-free outside and plenty of colors. It comes with a stainless-steel straw, a straw brush and an extra lid, and many owners seemed happy with how easy it is to clean, though some complained about the quality of the steel inside. At twenty-three dollars in 2020 it was hard to recommend over other options on the list, given its features and build.

**Now:** Greens Steel, which makes the Beast, listed the 20 oz tumbler at $23 when checked, in sizes from 10 to 50 oz. It describes vacuum insulation, 304 food-grade stainless steel that is electropolished inside, and a sliding, spill-resistant Tritan lid with a straw; it says its tumblers, lids and straws are dishwasher safe and offers a lifetime guarantee. It gives no rating in hours. To buy: [Greens Steel's store](https://greenssteel.com/products/beast-tumblers-1), or the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Beast+20+oz+Stainless+Steel+Tumbler&tag=jayarrcoffee-20" rel="sponsored noopener">Beast 20 oz Tumbler</a> on Amazon.

## To keep it at drinking temperature: a heated mug

### Ember Mug 2: our high-tech pick

**Our 2020 verdict:** the Ember is in a category of its own and can't really be compared with the other mugs here. It's a heated mug that you program from an app on your phone: you set the temperature you want, and it keeps your drink at exactly that temperature, the video said for about 80 minutes. Perhaps surprisingly, it's also very easy to wash by hand. It suits coffee brewed at a lower temperature, such as your own AeroPress recipe, by keeping it warm for longer. We recommended it as our high-end, ultra-tech pick, listed at $180 in our video.

**Now:** Ember's store page for the Mug 2 now opens its Ember Mug 3, which Ember calls "the mug you know and love" made "even better": you can set the temperature on the mug as well as in the app, it's top-rack dishwasher safe, and Ember says it keeps a drink warm for over three hours with its included sliding lid. Ember listed the Mug 3 at $129.95 for 10 oz and $149.95 for 14 oz, and still listed Mug 2 editions at $89.99 for 10 oz and $99.99 for 14 oz, when checked. For the Mug 2, Ember says it holds your chosen temperature for up to 1.5 hours in the 10 oz size and up to 80 minutes in the 14 oz size, and that it's safe to wash by hand and submersible to 1 meter. To buy: [Ember's store](https://ember.com/products/ember-mug-3), or the <a href="https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Ember+Temperature+Control+Smart+Mug+2&tag=jayarrcoffee-20" rel="sponsored noopener">Ember Mug 2</a> on Amazon, where the first results are ads, including one for the Mug 3.

## Filling it on the road

If you'd rather brew at the other end of the trip, an AeroPress packs small and makes one cup at a time. At its largest dose in our Brew Guide it makes about 264 ml, or 9 fl oz (calculated), so it fills a 12 oz mug about three-quarters full.

A full travel mug also carries more caffeine than a standard cup. Our Caffeine Calculator estimates that 20 fl oz of drip coffee brewed at our Brew Guide's strength has roughly 265 to 485 mg. The FDA's guidance reads: "For most adults, the FDA has cited 400 milligrams a day — that's about two to three 12-fluid-ounce cups of coffee — as an amount not generally associated with negative effects." It adds that "there is wide variation in both how sensitive people are to the effects of caffeine and how fast they eliminate it from the body." If you're pregnant, take medicines or have a condition caffeine might affect, ask your clinician what's right for you; our [caffeine guide](/blog/caffeine-in-coffee-guide/) has the details.
